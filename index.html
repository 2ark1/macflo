About this page

What the regimes mean

Cycle read: phases

  • Early cycle. Policy easing, the yield curve steepening, credit spreads tightening and growth bottoming out after a slowdown.
  • Mid cycle. Growth and earnings positive, credit expanding and volatility low.
  • Late cycle. Inflation rising, policy tightening, the curve flattening and the labour market tight.
  • Contraction. Earnings falling, credit tightening, unemployment rising and volatility spiking.
  • Unassigned. Not enough signals available, or no phase clearly ahead, so no phase is named.

Cycle read: growth / inflation

  • Goldilocks. Growth improving while inflation eases.
  • Inflationary boom. Growth and inflation both rising.
  • Stagflation. Growth slowing while inflation rises.
  • Deflationary bust. Growth and inflation both falling.

Numbers beside the label are growth and inflation momentum scaled against their own recent history (0 = flat). Sector tape is the phase that this week's sector ranking most resembles. Recession signals counts long-standing warning signs now showing, out of the five tracked that have data this week. "Data to" is the month of the oldest monthly input.
